About Arann Tech

Translating technical risk into business resilience.

Arann Tech is a boutique cybersecurity advisory firm built on one idea: business leaders deserve clear security advice, not jargon.

Firm Philosophy

Clear Advice. No Product Resale.

We bridge deep technical risk expertise and executive decision-making. We don't sell unnecessary tools or rely on fear.

We provide the governance, risk and compliance frameworks that help your organization grow securely and earn the trust of enterprise clients.

Defensible Cybersecurity: Built by certified practitioners for organizations that take trust seriously.

Jeneeit Jeyakanthan, CISSP

Founder & Managing Principal

12+ Years in Technology • Governance, Risk & Compliance Leadership

Jeneeit founded Arann Tech and leads its advisory practice. With more than 12 years in technology and a focus on governance, risk and compliance, he has designed identity and access management controls, led incident response operations, and managed enterprise risk registers in highly regulated environments.

His background in public-sector technical communications with the Legislative Assembly of Ontario shapes the firm's approach: turning dense statutory requirements and complex threat models into plain-language plans.
