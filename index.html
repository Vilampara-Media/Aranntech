The Problem

Security and compliance shouldn't be a guessing game.

For growing healthcare providers, financial firms and professional services businesses, a failed vendor audit or a single breach can stall deals, trigger regulators and disrupt operations.

You probably don't need a large outsourced SOC and thousands of noisy alerts. You need a dedicated team of senior advisors who build your policies, keep your risk register current, hold your vendors to account and explain it all to your board in plain language.

Audit readiness without last-minute scrambling
Defensible policies that reflect real-world operations
Vendor scrutiny before third parties jeopardize your data
Our Approach

Vendor-Neutral, Defensible Security

Arann Tech is a vendor-neutral advisory firm. We don't resell software or sell on fear. We design and maintain security programs aligned with ISO 27001 and NIST CSF so you can demonstrate control to auditors, clients and regulators.
