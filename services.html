Cybersecurity Advisory & GRC Services

Security by design. Compliance by default.

Security leadership retainers, risk assessments, threat modeling and third-party risk management for regulated organizations in Canada and the US.

Practice 01

Security Leadership Retainers

Security works best as an ongoing function, not a one-off project. Our retainers place a dedicated Arann Tech team behind your security program, with executive-level leadership at a fraction of the cost of building a security department.

What's included:

  • • A named lead and a Security Program Office. One accountable senior advisor plus analysts who handle the day-to-day work.
  • • Risk management. We build and maintain your enterprise risk register, track inherent and residual risk, and drive corrective action plans to closure.
  • • Policy architecture. Plain-English acceptable use, access control, incident response and other policies your staff will actually read.
  • • Compliance support. Gap assessments and audit preparation for ISO 27001, NIST CSF 2.0, SOC 2, PIPEDA and HIPAA.
  • • Board and executive reporting. Quarterly risk summaries and board-ready briefings.
  • • On-demand advisory. Help with client security questionnaires, RFPs and incident questions.
Best for: Organizations without a full-time security leader that face client, regulator or insurer scrutiny.
Practice 02

Risk Assessments & Threat Modeling

Before you launch a new application or migrate infrastructure, we examine how it's designed, not just whether a scanner finds known vulnerabilities.

  • • Structured methods. STRIDE and PASTA applied to data flows, authentication and network segmentation.
  • • Actionable output. A prioritized remediation plan written for the people who'll do the work, not a 100-page list of findings.
  • • Defined scope. Fixed-fee engagements with clear deliverables.
Best for: Product launches, cloud migrations, M&A due diligence and pre-audit reviews.
Practice 03

Third-Party Risk Management

Your risk includes your vendors' risk. We take vendor due diligence off your team's plate.

  • • SOC 2 and attestation reviews. We analyze Type 2 reports, including testing exceptions and Complementary User Entity Controls (CUECs), and explain what they mean for you.
  • • Questionnaire management. We issue and evaluate SIG and CAIQ questionnaires covering data residency, access control and encryption before contracts are signed.
  • • Ongoing oversight. Vendor inventory, tiering and periodic reassessment.
Best for: Organizations that rely on cloud and SaaS providers to handle sensitive or regulated data.
Practice 04

Compliance Readiness

A focused, time-boxed engagement to prepare for a specific audit or certification: SOC 2, ISO 27001, PIPEDA, HIPAA or a client-mandated assessment. Includes a gap assessment, remediation roadmap, evidence guidance and pre-audit review.

Gap Assessment

Comprehensive benchmarking against framework controls.

Remediation Roadmap

Prioritized tasks to eliminate control shortfalls.

Evidence Guidance

Exact documentation and logs required by auditors.

Pre-Audit Review

Mock audit walkthrough to ensure complete readiness.
