Executive Briefing

Clear Rules for Responsible AI Adoption

Your staff are probably already using generative AI, approved or not. Without clear rules, sensitive data can end up in public tools, and vendors may be using your information in ways you haven't agreed to.

Our AI governance practice helps you set practical guardrails so you can benefit from AI without creating privacy, legal or intellectual property exposure.

Framework Alignment: Scaled specifically to ISO 42001 (Artificial Intelligence Management System) and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.
