Why Arann Tech?

Your Risks. Our Expertise.

You’ve outgrown basic IT security — but you’re not ready to build a full internal security operations center. With growth comes complexity: more data, more systems, more regulatory exposure across US and Canadian jurisdictions, and more attention from threat actors who specifically target organizations your size. We specialize in four areas where mid-market organizations are most vulnerable and least prepared:

  1. Data that’s always recoverable
  2. Mergers that don’t inherit threats
  3. Operations that stay secure, 24/7
  4. Surveillance that stops threats before they escalate

No hype. No empty guarantees. Just proven solutions built for companies with 50–500 employees.

How We Work
Step 1

Discovery & Assessment

Our assessment typically spans three to five business days and includes stakeholder interviews, active system scans, and a structured review of your current security policies, infrastructure, and compliance obligations under applicable US and Canadian frameworks.

Step 2

Custom Security Plan

Based on what we learn, we design a tailored solution that aligns with your IT team’s capacity, business priorities, and compliance needs. We present clear recommendations, timelines, and milestones-no generic templates.

Step 3

Seamless Implementation

Our team manages the deployment from start to finish, coordinating with your staff to minimize disruption. Most solutions are fully implemented within two to four weeks, depending on project scope and your operational schedule.

Step 4

Ongoing Partnership

After rollout, we provide continuous monitoring, regular check-ins, and transparent reporting to ensure your security posture stays strong as your business evolves.
